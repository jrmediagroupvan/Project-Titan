# Module Map

## CRM
Customers, organizations, addresses, notes, activity, files, communication history.

## Quoting
Pricing profiles, materials, machine time, labour, overhead, margins, discounts, taxes, quote revisions, approval links, PDFs.

## AI STL Developer
Permission-aware prompt-to-parametric-design workflow, restricted geometry validation, binary STL compilation, revision history, interactive preview, customer-file export, audit records, and handoff to Bambu slicing and quoting.

## Orders
Order conversion, deposits, invoices, payment status, fulfilment status, refunds, cancellations.

## Production
Jobs, operations, queue, printer assignment, estimates, failures, reprints, quality control, maintenance.

## Inventory
Materials, spools, lots, suppliers, purchase orders, reservations, consumption, adjustments, low-stock alerts.

## Customer Portal
Uploads, quote approval, payment, order tracking, messaging, invoices, reorder.

## Platform
Authentication, roles, audit logs, notifications, settings, integrations, backups, reporting.
