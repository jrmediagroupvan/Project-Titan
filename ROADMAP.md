# TITAN Commercial Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Foundation
Authentication, roles, migrations, audit log, settings, installer, backups.

## Milestone 2 — Business Core
CRM, uploads, quoting, approvals, orders, production, inventory.

## Milestone 3 — Customer Portal
Accounts, secure links, payments, tracking, invoices, messaging.

## Milestone 4 — Integrations
Square, email, Canada Post, S3/MinIO, OpenAI.

## Milestone 5 — Print Farm
Bambu, Klipper, scheduling, telemetry, maintenance, spool consumption.

## Milestone 6 — Commercial Hardening
MFA, monitoring, rate limiting, recovery testing, compliance, automated test coverage, public deployment.
